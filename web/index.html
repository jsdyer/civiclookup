    02 / READ THE RECORD

    Useful contacts.
    Clear limits.

    A directory, not a voter-registration check

    Data comes from Represent by Open North, which collects public elected-official and boundary information. Coverage and freshness differ between sources. Missing results do not mean a place has no elected representatives.

    Postal codes can cross districts

    We combine both postal-centroid and official-concordance matches and label each one. Multiple districts or representatives can apply. Use explicit coordinates to narrow the geographic match, and verify eligibility with the relevant election authority.

    Your search stays yours

    No search is stored by this app. Open North receives the location you submit and the normal connection information described in its privacy policy. Contacts open only when you choose them. Saved receipts include your query; share them thoughtfully.
